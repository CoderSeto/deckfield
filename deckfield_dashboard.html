Process

Each round's pairings are generated as soon as the round before it is complete, so the newest section is the next one to be played. Draw: higher (better) seed hosts. Process: lower (worse) seed hosts. After round 5 both brackets converge on the Mutual Stage above: 4 distinct finalists means a Draw semifinal and a Process semifinal; if one team reached the final two on both sides it byes straight to the final and the other two play the lone semifinal. Semifinal and final are two-legged — the worse seed hosts leg 1, the better seed hosts leg 2 — and the pairing is shown in leg-2 orientation. Each cup has its own Mutual Stage block, shown above this bracket as soon as that cup’s round 5 is complete in both brackets.
160-team ladder format: seeds 97–160 play round 1, then 65–96 (round 2), 33–64 (round 3), and 1–32 (round 4) enter in turn. Rounds 5–8 are a standard bracket among the 32 row-champions (32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2), so each bracket sends two teams into a shared mutual semifinal, with the same duplicate-handling as the smaller cups for the semifinal and final. There is no mutual quarterfinal — round 8 is each bracket's own.
