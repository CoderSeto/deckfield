Weighted sum uses multipliers Rank ×2, OVR ×2, Wins ×1, DSCR ×2, Elo ×1, TOT ×1, GI ×1, then a tanh curve (k = 2× std dev) maps it to a 0.25–1.75 multiplier centered on 1.0.

Process

Each round's pairings are generated as soon as the round before it is complete, so the newest section is the next one to be played. Draw: higher (better) seed hosts. Process: lower (worse) seed hosts. After round 5 both brackets converge on the Mutual Stage above: 4 distinct finalists means a Draw semifinal and a Process semifinal; if one team reached the final two on both sides it byes straight to the final and the other two play the lone semifinal. Semifinal and final are two-legged — the worse seed hosts leg 1, the better seed hosts leg 2 — so each leg gets its own row, in that leg’s own orientation, with the combined AGG line beneath deciding the tie (an exact aggregate tie goes to the better seed). Each cup has its own Mutual Stage block, shown above this bracket as soon as that cup’s round 5 is complete in both brackets.
160-team ladder format: seeds 97–160 play round 1, then 65–96 (round 2), 33–64 (round 3), and 1–32 (round 4) enter in turn. Rounds 5–8 are a standard bracket among the 32 row-champions (32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2), so each bracket sends two teams into a shared mutual semifinal, with the same duplicate-handling as the smaller cups for the semifinal and final. There is no mutual quarterfinal — round 8 is each bracket's own. The semifinal is two-legged like the smaller cups' — worse seed hosts leg 1, better seed hosts leg 2, AGG decides — and a team's seed there is the better of its Draw and Process seeds. The final is a best of three: better seed hosts game 1, worse seed game 2, and game 3 — played only at 1–1 — goes to whoever leads on aggregate.

MD2/3 and MD4/5: two-legged ties, worse seed hosts leg 1, better seed hosts leg 2 -- MD6/7 (semifinal) and MD8/9 (final) follow the same host rule. Only rounds with real prior results are shown. Played games show their score with the winner in region colour, and the second leg of each tie adds an AGG line — the aggregate decides who advances (an exact tie goes to the higher seed). These are Playoff games: they count toward the P/F record, TOT and OVR, never toward the Regional record or standings. Every RT game carries a Factor Modifier for the higher seed: its Regional Points (RP) minus its opponent’s, divided by 4 in rounds 1–2 (MD1–MD3), 8 in round 3 (MD4/5), 12 in the semifinal (MD6/7) and 16 in the final (MD8/9) — it arrives in DECKFIELD as the matchup’s Adv. Bonus points toward TOT: 15 for reaching the semifinal (paid when MD5 ends), 30 for reaching the final (when the semifinal ends), 45 for the regional champion (when the final ends).
