DECKFIELD

Mock-up only — a standalone page, nothing is wired into the game. Real season-9 data (Camphrier Town at Canalave City). One axis runs the full width and every column hangs off it: away above, home below. Accolades split into rows on semicolons; rank sits to the left of the name; both names hug the centre. Records sit outboard (top and bottom); typing, ATK and Every X sit inboard in team colour, with the “P Type / S Type” labels dropped. Stat labels sit outboard of their numbers, so the values meet at the axis. The spread sits in brackets left of the favourite's name, in that team's colour, with the team label dropped. Score is stacked away-over-home; the names and score sit left to make room for the time block on their right: round name on top, then period over clock over target, weather along the bottom. Ticks read as a clock face — 502 → 50:20, target 500+35 → 53:50. Every column is a locked px width, so a three-digit score, a long team name or a long round name can never shift the banner.
